How to download TikTok videos

Updated 19 November 2026 · 3 minute read

TikTok's own Save video button is often turned off by the creator, and when it works it adds an end card. Here is how to save a TikTok as a plain MP4 file, or keep only the sound, with SIDM.

Quick answer

On Android, tap Share on the TikTok, choose SIDM (under More or Other) and tap Download. On a computer, open the TikTok in your browser with the SIDM extension, play it and click Download on the video.

On a computer (Chrome, Edge or Brave)

  1. Install SIDM in your browser

    Add the free SIDM extension to Chrome, Edge or Brave.

  2. Open the TikTok on tiktok.com

    Open the video's own page (click it in the feed so the address shows /video/). Signing in is not always needed, but some videos are only shown to signed-in viewers.

  3. Play and click Download

    Let it play for a moment, then click the Download button on the video and choose a quality or audio only.

  4. Find the file

    The video is saved as an MP4 in Downloads\SIDM\Video. Click it in the SIDM popup to open it.

On an Android phone

Don't have SIDM on your phone yet? Install SIDM for Android first. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open the TikTok

    Find the video in the TikTok app.

  2. Share to SIDM

    Tap Share (the arrow), then More or Other, and pick SIDM. Or tap Copy link and paste the link into SIDM.

  3. Pick MP4 or MP3

    Choose a video quality, or Audio only (MP3) for just the sound, and tap Download. It lands in Downloads/SIDM.

Tips for TikTok videos

  • Save the sound for a project? Audio only gives you an MP3 you can use in your own edits, if you have the rights to that sound.
  • Photo slideshows are not videos, so there is no video to download from them.
  • Region-locked or removed TikToks can't be saved: if the app shows "video unavailable", SIDM can't fetch it either.
  • Many at once? On Android, share several TikToks to SIDM one after another; they queue and download in the background.

Download only what you have the right to. Save videos you made, videos the owner allows you to keep, or videos under a licence that permits it. SIDM never downloads DRM-protected content such as Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ or Spotify. SIDM is not affiliated with any site it works with.

Questions

Can I download a TikTok when the Save button is turned off?

SIDM fetches the video the same way the player does, so it works for videos you can watch. Respect the creator's wishes and copyright: if they turned off saving, ask them before you reuse their video.

Can I download TikTok audio as MP3?

Yes. On Android choose Audio only (MP3). On a computer choose audio only in the quality menu.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. SIDM is available for Chrome, Edge and Brave on computers and for Android phones. A web app for iPhone is planned.

Is it free?

Yes. SIDM is free with no account and no ads.
