How to download Instagram reels

Updated 19 November 2026 · 3 minute read

Instagram lets you save reels inside the app, but not to your phone or computer. This guide shows how to download Instagram reels and video posts as normal MP4 files you can keep, edit or watch offline.

Quick answer

On Android, tap Share on the reel and choose SIDM, then pick a quality. On a computer, open the reel in Chrome, Edge or Brave with the SIDM extension, play it and click Download on the video.

On a computer (Chrome, Edge or Brave)

  1. Install SIDM in your browser

    Add the free SIDM extension to Chrome, Edge or Brave. You only do this once.

  2. Open the reel on instagram.com

    Sign in to Instagram in your browser and open the reel or video post. Opening it on its own page (not in the feed) gives the most reliable result.

  3. Play it, then click Download

    Let the reel play for a moment, then click the Download button on the video. SIDM reads the qualities from Instagram's own data, often in a higher resolution than the player shows.

  4. Choose a quality

    Pick the highest quality or audio only. The file is saved as an MP4 in Downloads\SIDM\Video.

On an Android phone

Don't have SIDM on your phone yet? Install SIDM for Android first. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open the reel in Instagram

    Find the reel or video post you want.

  2. Tap Share, then SIDM

    Tap the Share (paper plane) icon, then Share to… or More, and pick SIDM. You can also tap Copy link and paste it in SIDM.

  3. Download

    Choose a quality and tap Download. The reel is saved to Downloads/SIDM and appears in your Gallery.

Tips for Instagram reels

  • Carousel posts with several videos: open the post and play the video you want before clicking Download, so SIDM picks that one.
  • Private accounts: you can only save reels you can watch while signed in. If SIDM for Android says a post needs you to sign in, open it in SIDM's built-in browser, sign in there, and tap Download.
  • Want only the music? Choose audio only (MP3 on Android) to keep just the sound of a reel.
  • Stories disappear after 24 hours, and Instagram does not always expose them the same way as reels. Reels and video posts are the reliable case.

Download only what you have the right to. Save videos you made, videos the owner allows you to keep, or videos under a licence that permits it. SIDM never downloads DRM-protected content such as Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ or Spotify. SIDM is not affiliated with any site it works with.

Questions

Can I download Instagram reels without an app?

On a computer, yes: the SIDM browser extension is enough. On a phone, SIDM for Android is the app that saves the file to your Gallery.

Does the downloaded reel keep its sound?

Yes. SIDM joins the video and audio into one MP4. Choose audio only if you want just the sound.

Will the creator know I downloaded their reel?

SIDM only fetches the video the same way the Instagram player does; it doesn't post anything or interact with the account. Please respect the creator's rights and only save reels you're allowed to keep.

Is SIDM free?

Yes, with no account, ads or tracking.
