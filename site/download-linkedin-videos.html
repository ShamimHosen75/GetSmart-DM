How to download LinkedIn videos

Updated 19 November 2026 · 3 minute read

LinkedIn doesn't let you download videos from posts, even your own once they're published. Here is how to save a LinkedIn video with its sound as one MP4 file.

Quick answer

Open the post on linkedin.com in Chrome, Edge or Brave with the SIDM extension, play the video, click Download on it and pick a quality. On Android, share the post to SIDM.

On a computer (Chrome, Edge or Brave)

  1. Install SIDM in your browser

    Add the free SIDM extension.

  2. Open the post

    Sign in to LinkedIn and open the post with the video. Opening it on its own page (from the post's menu, Copy link to post, then paste it in the address bar) works best in a long feed.

  3. Play and click Download

    Start the video and click Download on it. LinkedIn sends one MP4 with both picture and sound, in parts; SIDM saves it whole.

  4. Pick a quality

    Choose the highest quality or a smaller one. The video is saved in Downloads\SIDM\Video.

On an Android phone

Don't have SIDM on your phone yet? Install SIDM for Android first. It takes about a minute.

  1. Copy the post link

    In the LinkedIn app, tap Share or the … menu on the post, then Copy link, or choose SIDM if it is in the share list.

  2. Paste it into SIDM

    Open SIDM and paste the link. If LinkedIn asks you to sign in, open the link in SIDM's built-in browser, sign in there and tap Download.

  3. Download

    Pick a quality. The video goes to Downloads/SIDM.

Tips for LinkedIn videos

  • Your own videos: downloading your post is a quick way to get a copy back if you lost the original file.
  • Webinars and live events can be saved after they end, once LinkedIn shows the replay.
  • LinkedIn Learning courses are paid, protected content and are not supported.
  • Documents and carousels in posts are PDFs, not videos. Use the post's own download option for those.

Download only what you have the right to. Save videos you made, videos the owner allows you to keep, or videos under a licence that permits it. SIDM never downloads DRM-protected content such as Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ or Spotify. SIDM is not affiliated with any site it works with.

Questions

Does the LinkedIn video keep its sound?

Yes. LinkedIn's video file already contains the sound, and SIDM saves it as one MP4.

Do I need to be signed in?

Usually yes. LinkedIn shows most posts only to signed-in members.

Can I download LinkedIn Learning videos?

No. Paid course content is protected, and SIDM doesn't support it.

Is it free?

Yes. SIDM is free, with no account and no ads.
