How to download Vimeo videos

Updated 19 November 2026 · 3 minute read

Some Vimeo videos have a download button and many don't, especially when they're embedded on another website. This guide shows how to save a Vimeo video you can watch, on vimeo.com or on any site that embeds it.

Quick answer

First check for Vimeo's own Download button under the video. If there isn't one, play the video in Chrome, Edge or Brave with the SIDM extension and click Download on it.

On a computer (Chrome, Edge or Brave)

  1. Install SIDM in your browser

    Add the free SIDM extension.

  2. Open the video

    Open it on vimeo.com, or on the website where it is embedded. SIDM works inside embedded players too.

  3. Play and click Download

    Start the video, then click Download on the player. SIDM lists the qualities Vimeo offers.

  4. Pick a quality

    Choose one. The MP4 is saved in Downloads\SIDM\Video.

On an Android phone

Don't have SIDM on your phone yet? Install SIDM for Android first. It takes about a minute.

  1. Copy the link

    Copy the address of the Vimeo page, or share it from your browser.

  2. Paste it into SIDM

    Open SIDM and paste the link, or share the page to SIDM. For videos embedded on another site, open that site in SIDM's built-in browser, play the video and tap Download.

  3. Download

    Pick a quality and tap Download.

Tips for Vimeo videos

  • Use Vimeo's own button when it's there. If the owner allows downloads, Vimeo's button gives you the original file.
  • Password-protected videos: enter the password in the player first, then download.
  • Vimeo On Demand rentals and purchases that are copy-protected are not supported.
  • Private links (unlisted) work when you have the link and can play the video.

Download only what you have the right to. Save videos you made, videos the owner allows you to keep, or videos under a licence that permits it. SIDM never downloads DRM-protected content such as Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ or Spotify. SIDM is not affiliated with any site it works with.

Questions

Can I download an embedded Vimeo video?

Yes, if you can play it. Open the page that embeds it, play it and click Download on the player.

Can I download Vimeo On Demand videos?

Not when they're copy-protected. SIDM never works around DRM.

Which quality will I get?

The qualities Vimeo serves for that video; choose the highest or a smaller one.
