How to download Facebook videos

Updated 19 November 2026 · 3 minute read

Facebook has no download button for most videos. Here is how to save a Facebook video, reel or Watch clip to your computer or phone in the best quality Facebook offers, for free.

Quick answer

On a computer, install the free SIDM extension, play the Facebook video for a few seconds, click the Download button that appears on it and pick a quality. On Android, tap Share on the video and choose SIDM.

On a computer (Chrome, Edge or Brave)

  1. Install SIDM in your browser

    Get the free SIDM extension for Chrome, Edge or Brave and load it as shown on the home page. You only do this once.

  2. Open the video on Facebook

    Go to the post, reel or Watch page in your browser while signed in to Facebook, and press play. Let it run for a few seconds so SIDM can see which qualities Facebook sends.

  3. Click Download on the video

    Move the pointer over the video. A Download button appears in its corner. Click it to see the qualities found, with the highest first.

  4. Pick a quality

    Choose HD, SD or audio only. SIDM fetches the pieces and joins picture and sound into one MP4 in Downloads\SIDM\Video. The menu shows the progress until it says Saved.

On an Android phone

Don't have SIDM on your phone yet? Install SIDM for Android first. It takes about a minute.

  1. Find the video in the Facebook app

    Open the reel or video post you want to keep.

  2. Share it to SIDM

    Tap Share, then look for SIDM in the list. If it is not there, tap More or Copy link and paste the link into SIDM.

  3. Choose the quality

    SIDM lists the qualities with the original first. Tap one and then Download. The video is saved to Downloads/SIDM and shows up in your Gallery.

Tips for Facebook videos

  • The button is on the video under your pointer. In a busy feed, open the post on its own page (click the time stamp) so SIDM matches the right video.
  • No Download button? Play the video first. Facebook loads videos only when they start, so SIDM needs a few seconds of playback.
  • Private and group videos work only if you can watch them while signed in. SIDM saves what your browser can play; it can't open videos you don't have access to.
  • Live videos can be saved after the broadcast ends, not while they are live.

Download only what you have the right to. Save videos you made, videos the owner allows you to keep, or videos under a licence that permits it. SIDM never downloads DRM-protected content such as Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ or Spotify. SIDM is not affiliated with any site it works with.

Questions

Is it free to download Facebook videos with SIDM?

Yes. SIDM is free, with no account, no ads and no limit on the number of videos.

Can I download Facebook videos in HD?

Yes. SIDM offers every quality Facebook sends for that video, highest first. If a video was uploaded in SD, HD isn't available.

Do I need to install a program on Windows?

No. For Facebook the browser extension does everything on its own. The optional Windows desktop program is only needed for YouTube and very fast big-file downloads.

Can I save a Facebook reel to my phone's Gallery?

Yes. With SIDM for Android, share the reel to SIDM and it lands in Downloads/SIDM, which your Gallery shows.
