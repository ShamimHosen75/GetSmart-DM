How to download YouTube videos

Updated 19 November 2026 · 3 minute read

YouTube scrambles its video streams, so most browser extensions can't save them. SIDM can, with its free Windows desktop program or the Android app. Read the note on YouTube's terms below before you start.

Quick answer

On Windows, install the SIDM extension and the free SIDM desktop program, then click Download on any YouTube video and pick a quality (each shows its size). On Android, share the video to SIDM.

On a computer (Chrome, Edge or Brave)

  1. Install the SIDM extension

    Load the free SIDM extension in Chrome, Edge or Brave.

  2. Install the SIDM desktop program

    YouTube needs the SIDM desktop program for Windows. In SIDM Settings click Install SIDM for Windows, run it and click Install. No admin rights needed. You only do this once.

  3. Click Download on the video

    Open the video or Short and click Download on it. The menu shows each quality with its file size, for example "≈ 98 MB". The first time, SIDM shows a short note about YouTube's terms.

  4. Choose a quality and a folder

    Click a quality, pick a folder in the window that opens, and follow the progress in the menu until it says Saved.

On an Android phone

Don't have SIDM on your phone yet? Install SIDM for Android first. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open the video in YouTube

    Find the video or Short.

  2. Share to SIDM

    Tap Share, then choose SIDM from the list (it may be under More).

  3. Pick a quality

    Choose a video quality or Audio only (MP3) and tap Download. The file goes to Downloads/SIDM.

Tips for YouTube videos

  • YouTube's terms allow downloads only where YouTube shows a download button. Only save videos you own, that are under a licence that allows it (for example Creative Commons), or that you have permission to save.
  • Mac and Linux: the desktop program has a Mac and Linux version too; see the FAQ on the home page.
  • A video stopped working? On Windows, run Update SIDM tools from the Start menu. The Android app updates its video engine by itself every day.
  • Members-only, paid and age-restricted videos that need a login may fail, and live streams can't be saved while live.

Download only what you have the right to. Save videos you made, videos the owner allows you to keep, or videos under a licence that permits it. SIDM never downloads DRM-protected content such as Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ or Spotify. SIDM is not affiliated with any site it works with.

Questions

Is it legal to download YouTube videos?

It depends on the video and where you live. YouTube's Terms of Service don't allow downloading except where YouTube offers a download button, so only save videos you own or have permission to save. You are responsible for following those terms.

Why do I need the desktop program for YouTube?

YouTube scrambles and signs its video streams. The desktop program uses yt-dlp, an open-source tool, to read them. Other sites work with the extension alone.

Can I download YouTube Shorts?

Yes, the same way as normal videos.

Can I turn YouTube downloads off?

Yes, in SIDM Settings on both the extension and the Android app.
